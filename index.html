Our Service Policy & Guidelines

We are here to solve your shopping inconveniences. Here is what you need to know.

Who We Serve

  • Tourists: We help you find souvenirs, essentials, and local goods at fair prices.
  • Locals: We run errands for busy families and professionals.
  • Fees: You pay for item cost + service fee + delivery.

Exclusions (We do NOT buy)

  • Medicine: For your safety, please buy these personally.
