Service Policy & Guidelines

Everything you need to know before ordering.

Who We Serve

  • Tourists: Souvenirs, essentials, local goods at fair prices.
  • Locals: Errands for busy families and professionals.
  • Fees: Item cost + service fee + delivery.

We Do NOT Buy

  • Medicine — buy personally for safety.
  • Alcohol — not handled.
  • Illegal products — strictly refused.
  • Large electronics (TVs, fridges) — small ones fine.

Essential Tips

  • Be specific — exact brands, sizes, colors.
  • Payment: M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Cash.
  • Min order: 50,000 TZS. Below = 5,000 TZS fee.
  • Receipt: Reference only — your order gets delivered.
